NOODLE

Ten questions.

One shared quiz each day. Pick one answer per question. Each correct answer is worth one point. No speed bonuses or wagers.

Your first answers save as you go. Come back to finish, then copy your spoiler-free score to the chat. The next edition is scheduled for midnight Eastern.

This is a friendly browser-local game, not an anti-cheat system. Clearing browser data or using another device resets your local record. Nothing is sent to a leaderboard.

 ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Context matters.

The private index covers the 2022–2025 archive.

That's the search corpus—not a claim that every message has been editorially reviewed. Each quiz is written from selected conversations, with replies and corrections checked. Receipts preserve original spelling. A message proves what somebody posted, not that the story really happened.

Only selected questions and short supporting excerpts are published. The full archive, contact details, attachments and research index stay off this website. Crude adult and bathroom humor included.

Scores and progress stay in this browser. The shared is.gd address is a third-party redirect to this site; no chat data is sent to the shortener.
